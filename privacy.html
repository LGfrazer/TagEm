Privacy Policy for Tag'Em

Effective date: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tag'Em ("Tag'Em", "the app", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Tag'Em Android application.

Developer / publisher: OddlyUsefulThings
Privacy contact: Use the developer contact details shown on Tag'Em's Google Play Store listing.

1. What Tag'Em does

Tag'Em is a photo organisation utility that lets you select photos on your Android device and rename them so they are easier to identify and search for later using your device's normal file or gallery search features.

2. Photos and files

Tag'Em may access photos, media files, filenames and related file information that you choose to make available to the app in order to provide its renaming and organisation features.

Photo content selected for Tag'Em's core renaming features is processed locally on your device. Tag'Em does not upload your photos to a server operated by the developer, and the developer does not use the contents of your photos for advertising or profiling.

Depending on your Android version and the feature being used, access may be provided through Android's system photo picker, storage/media permissions, or another Android system file-selection mechanism. You can control relevant permissions through Android settings.

3. Information stored locally on your device

Tag'Em may store app settings and operational information locally on your device, such as language preferences, tutorial/onboarding status, feature settings, counters used to manage app behaviour, and whether an ad-free entitlement is available. This information is generally removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to any information separately retained by third-party services such as Google Play.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of Tag'Em may display advertising provided through Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Google's advertising services may automatically collect or receive certain information from your device, including your IP address, interactions with the app or advertisements, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available. Google may use this information for purposes including advertising, ad measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Where required by law, Tag'Em uses Google's consent tools to ask for your choices before personalised advertising or related storage/access technologies are used. You may be able to change or withdraw your advertising consent choices through the privacy options made available within the app or through your device and Google settings.

Advertising SDKs can use device identifiers and local storage technologies that perform functions similar to cookies. Tag'Em's privacy-policy page itself does not intentionally use advertising tags or tracking scripts.

5. Tag'Em Pro and payments

If you purchase Tag'Em Pro or another paid entitlement through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. Tag'Em does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank details. The app may receive information needed to confirm and restore your purchase, such as purchase status, product information, transaction identifiers or purchase tokens.

6. Third-party services

Tag'Em may use Google services including Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), Google Play services and Google Play Billing. These services may process information under their own privacy terms.

You can read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and information about Google's advertising technologies at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

7. How information is used

Information handled by Tag'Em or its service providers may be used to:

  • provide photo renaming, organisation and app functionality;
  • remember your settings and app preferences;
  • show, measure and manage advertising in the free version;
  • record and respect advertising/privacy choices;
  • verify paid or ad-free entitlements;
  • maintain security, diagnose faults and prevent fraud or abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Legal bases for processing

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, processing may rely on one or more lawful bases depending on the activity. Where consent is legally required for personalised advertising or related device storage/access, processing is based on your consent. Necessary app operation, security and fraud-prevention activities may rely on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or legal obligations as applicable.

9. Sharing of information

The developer does not sell your photos or personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to provide features described in this policy, including Google in connection with advertising, consent management, app distribution and billing. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights, safety or the security of the app.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because Tag'Em's core photo-renaming activity takes place locally, the developer does not retain copies of your selected photos on a developer-operated server.

Local app data remains on your device until it is changed, cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Information processed by Google or other third-party services is retained according to those providers' own policies and applicable legal requirements.

If you contact the developer directly, correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

11. International data transfers

Third-party providers such as Google may process information in countries outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using appropriate legal safeguards for international transfers under applicable data-protection law.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including rights to access, correct, erase or restrict certain processing, object to certain processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Because Tag'Em's core features are designed to work locally and the developer may not hold identifiable information about you, there may be cases where the developer has no server-side personal data that can be retrieved or deleted.

To make a privacy request, use the developer contact details shown on Tag'Em's Google Play Store listing. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/.

13. Children

Tag'Em is not specifically designed for children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated account or server. If Tag'Em's target audience or features change, this policy and the app's privacy settings will be updated as required.

14. Security

Tag'Em is designed to minimise developer-side collection by processing its core photo-renaming functionality locally on your device. Third-party services used by the app apply their own technical and organisational safeguards. No method of electronic processing or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
